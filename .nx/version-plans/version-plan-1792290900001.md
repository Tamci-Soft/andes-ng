Add `AndesHoverCard` with `andesHoverCardTrigger` and `andesHoverCardContent`: a preview card that opens when the pointer rests on its trigger or keyboard focus reaches it (`openDelay` 600ms, `closeDelay` 300ms), stays open while the pointer crosses an invisible bridge onto the card or while focus is inside it, and closes on Escape. The trigger is described by the card rather than controlling a dialog; `Alt+ArrowDown` on the trigger moves focus into the card's links and buttons, and a tap toggles the card on touch screens. It supports `side`/`align`/`placement`, offsets, an optional arrow, an `[(open)]` model, and a 4px drift and fade on enter and exit.
