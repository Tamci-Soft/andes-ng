Add an elevation scale (`--andes-shadow-sm`/`-md`/`-lg`), `--andes-color-primary-subtle` for selected rows and date ranges, `--andes-color-{success,info}-solid` (with `-hover`/`-active`) as aliases of the success/info fills, and the shorter motion names `--andes-duration-fast`/`-base`/`-slow` and `--andes-ease-out`/`-in` as aliases of the `--andes-motion-*` tokens. Motion tokens settle at 120/150/200ms with a `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` ease-out and a `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)` ease-in, and `--andes-focus-halo` is a single 2px ring so a focused field shows one indicator. `--andes-color-danger-text` is `#b91c1c` on light surfaces (5.9:1 on the muted surface), and `--andes-color-warning-foreground` deepens to `#451a03` (7:1 on the amber fill, 4.7:1 on hover). Both themes keep `-foreground` at 4.5:1 on their fills and `-text` at 4.5:1 on the page, card, popover and muted surfaces; the token tests assert the final values and ratios.
