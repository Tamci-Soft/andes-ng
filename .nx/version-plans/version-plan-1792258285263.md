Toasts and messages accept template content (`titleTemplate`/`messageTemplate`, `contentTemplate`), inline `style` and root `attributes`, can drop the close button with `closeIcon: null`, and gain a per-flavor `rtl` option for right-to-left content.

Toasts are redesigned as neutral cards whose severity shows in a coloured icon (or, with the new `richColors` option, a tinted card), with compact `action`/new `cancel` buttons and a corner close badge; notifications now collapse into a deck by default (`stack: true` collapses from two toasts, `visibleToasts` sets how many cards show) that expands and pauses every countdown on hover, slide in and out from their edge with height-aware reflow, and can be swiped away (`swipeToDismiss`, `swipeDirections`, close reason `'swipe'`), with new `gap` and `offset` options.
