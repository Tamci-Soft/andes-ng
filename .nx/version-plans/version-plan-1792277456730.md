AndesAccordion resizes to a 14px / 22px header with 12px 16px padding (8px 12px at sm, 16px 24px at lg), aligned panel padding, and a collapse and chevron turn on the motion tokens (200ms). The arrow always sits in one column at the row's end inset, after the extra (before the title with `expandIconPosition="start"`), so rows with extras of different widths line up; `showArrow=false` rows keep an empty arrow-sized box, and a nested accordion runs flush with its parent panel so its arrows and titles line up with the parent's. Every header of a given size is the same height (46px at md, 38px at sm, 56px at lg) whatever its extra holds: the height comes from a per-size minimum with the content centered, so a button in the extra no longer makes its row taller.
