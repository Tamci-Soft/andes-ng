Add Menubar: a horizontal desktop-style bar of menus (File, Edit, View...) whose triggers open dropdown panels built from the Dropdown Menu items, submenus, checkbox and radio parts. Once one menu is open, pointing at another trigger switches menus with no extra click, Left/Right move between triggers and across open menus (mirrored in RTL), and Escape returns focus to the trigger.
