Dropdown menu: link items (`href`/`target`/`rel`), a menu-wide and per-item `closeOnSelect`, a `popupTemplate` that wraps the menu in custom popup content, `panelClass`, per-submenu `popupOffset`/`popupClass` and a `(submenuTriggerClick)` output. The dropdown button gains `loadingDelay` and `loadingIcon`.

The dropdown menu panel enters with a 150ms fade and a 4px drift and leaves with a quicker one while marked as closing, and group labels use the 12px caption size on a 20px line (rows stay 32px).
