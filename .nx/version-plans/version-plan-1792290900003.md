Add `AndesCommand`, a searchable command list, and `AndesCommandDialog`, which opens it in a modal overlay. A search box over items grouped under headings is filtered and ranked as you type by a fuzzy scorer (every word must match, accents and case ignored, `keywords` per item, matched letters highlighted), or shown as given with `shouldFilter` off for server-side results. Items carry icons, shortcuts shown as key caps, disabled state and nested pages (`pages` stack, Backspace in an empty box or Escape goes back); the list has empty and loading states, wrapping arrow-key navigation with Home and End, `aria-activedescendant` combobox semantics, and keeps the active item in view. The dialog toggles with `Mod+K` (configurable with `hotkey`), traps focus, fades its scrim and scales in 0.97 to 1 over 150ms, and animates out on close.
