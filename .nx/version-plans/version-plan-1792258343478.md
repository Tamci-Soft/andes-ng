Breadcrumb `[items]` crumbs gain a leading `icon` (with `ariaLabel` for icon-only crumbs, also used in the collapse dropdown), and crumb menus gain dividers, labelled groups, per-entry `key`/`icon`/`danger`/`target`, plus `dropdownProps` (panel `side`/`align`, `panelClass`, `disabled`, `onOpenChange`) and a `(menuOpenChange)` output.

The breadcrumb trail now sits on the 22px body line height instead of the font default.
