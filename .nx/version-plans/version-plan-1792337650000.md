Add viewport primitives: `andesElementSize` and `andesContainerBreakpoint` (element and container-width signals on one shared, frame-coalesced `ResizeObserver`), `andesMediaQuery` with reduced-motion, dark and contrast helpers, and an `AndesBreakpoints` service with `active`, `up`, `down` and `between` signals. All are server-safe and clean up with their injector.
