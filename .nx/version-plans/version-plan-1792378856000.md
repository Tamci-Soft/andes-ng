Upgrade `andes-carousel`: autoplay is now an explicit state machine (running, or paused by hover, keyboard focus, a drag, the user, a hidden tab or scrolling out of view, each resuming on its own with a full delay; reduced motion turns it off) with a play/pause button, an options object and `autoplayState` / `isPlaying`; new dot styles (`dot`, `line`, `pill`), sizes, placements on all four edges inside or outside the slides and colour custom properties; `orientation`, drag with `draggable`, `dragFree`, `dragThreshold` and `skipSnaps`, wheel scrolling, `slide-fade` effect with `speed`, `easing` and `waitForAnimate`, `slidesToScroll`, breakpoint `slidesPerView`, `align`, `peek`, `containScroll`, `adaptiveHeight`, `aspectRatio`, `startIndex`, outside and custom arrows, the `andes-carousel-thumbs` strip, the `selectedIndex`, `slideCount`, `canScrollPrev`, `canScrollNext` signals and the `slideChange`, `slideChanged`, `dragStarted`, `dragEnded`, `settled` and `ready` outputs (`beforeChange`, `afterChange` and the dot `dotPosition` placement are renamed).
