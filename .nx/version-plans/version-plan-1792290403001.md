Add `AndesColorPicker`: a swatch trigger that opens a panel with a saturation/brightness area, hue and opacity sliders, HEX/RGB/HSB fields, labelled preset groups and an eyedropper where the browser has one. It reads and writes colour text or an `AndesColor` (parsing, formatting and conversions are plain TypeScript, no dependencies), works with Angular forms, and reports `change`, `changeComplete`, `clear` and `openChange`.
