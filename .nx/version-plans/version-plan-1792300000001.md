Add Context Menu: a menu that opens at the pointer on a right-click, under a held finger on touch, or at the focused element from the keyboard (Shift+F10 or the context-menu key), using the Dropdown Menu items, submenus and checkbox/radio parts. The native menu is replaced only inside its areas, the panel flips and shifts to stay inside the viewport, and one menu can serve many areas, reporting which one was used.
