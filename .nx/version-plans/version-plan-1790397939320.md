AndesSelect's default size is now 32px high with 14px text (sm 24px, lg 40px), with tags and panel options scaled to match.

AndesSelect also gains `fieldNames` for options in any shape, `filterSort`, `defaultActiveFirstOption`, `maxTagCount="responsive"` (tags fitted to one row), `panelHeader`/`panelFooter` templates, a numeric or boolean `matchTriggerWidth` on the select itself, `loadingIcon` and `optionSelectedIcon`, option and group `title` tooltips, and the `searchInput`, `focused`, `blurred`, `activeOptionChange`, `inputKeydown` and `panelScroll` outputs; `listHeight` now caps the scrolling option list, so header and footer content stays in view.

AndesSelect's box now follows the shared field look: a tinted border on hover and, on focus or while open, a primary border with a soft 2px ring in place of the outline around the border (red or amber with a status); disabled boxes are muted instead of faded. The panel always opens below or above the box, never beside it, and is shortened to the room available instead of covering the box; it fades in from 97% scale over 150ms. An empty multiple or tags box starts its placeholder and caret where a single select's text starts, tags use the box's 14px text (12px in `sm`), and group labels are 12px.

The clear button now has one design shared with the other fields - a small muted disc with the "x" knocked out, 1em across with a 24px hit area, swapped in for the chevron while the box is hovered or focused (always on touch screens) - and is now a Tab stop of its own with a focus ring, named by the new `clearLabel` input (default "Clear"); the panel also animates out (120ms fade to 97% scale, mirroring its entrance) when the overlay marks it closing, and instantly with reduced motion.
