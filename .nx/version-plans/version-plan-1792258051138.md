Popover and Popconfirm gain a `color` input (semantic presets or any CSS color, with automatic text contrast), Popover gains a public `close()`, and `[andesPopoverClose]` now also works from template content passed to the inputs, and Popconfirm gains a `popupClick` output.

Popover titles are now 16px semibold (Popconfirm titles semibold at 14px), and the panel enters in 150ms on a curve without overshoot.

Popover and Popconfirm gain a `maxHeight` input that scrolls the content below the title (the title stays pinned and the arrow is never clipped), a Popover body under a title is set in the muted foreground so the title leads, and both panels now fade and drift back toward their trigger when they close. The success, info and warning colour presets now meet 4.5:1 text contrast.
