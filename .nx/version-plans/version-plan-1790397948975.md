AndesTabs: the editable-card add button shrinks from 36px to 32px along the bar (width in horizontal bars, height in vertical ones), matching the 32px default control size. Unselected card tabs now show a hover fill, and the overflow scroll arrows of a horizontal bar mirror in right-to-left text.

AndesTabs also gains an overflow menu: once the tabs overflow, a "more" button lists the tabs scrolled out of view (`more`, `moreIcon`, `moreLabel`, `moreTrigger` for click or hover, `moreSearch` for a filter field, and `moreTemplate` for custom menu content); draggable tabs (`draggable`, with `Alt`+`Shift`+arrow key as the keyboard alternative) that report each move through the new `(reorder)` output for the consumer to apply; and `tabBarStyle`/`tabBarClass` for styling the tab bar, such as making it sticky. Reordering tabs in the DOM now keeps arrow-key navigation and the ink bar in the new order.

Tab panels now cross-fade in 150ms instead of sliding in over 300ms, the ink bar moves in 200ms, the overflow menu opens with the shared gentle scale-and-fade, its filter field uses the standard field look (primary border and soft ring on focus, no outline frame).

An overflowing tab bar now fades out toward each edge that still hides tabs (start/end follow the reading direction; top/bottom in vertical bars), the overflow menu closes with a short 120ms fade-and-scale (instant with reduced motion), and card tabs read their surfaces from `--andes-tabs-card-selected-background` and `--andes-tabs-card-background`, so a container such as a card can match them to its own surface.
