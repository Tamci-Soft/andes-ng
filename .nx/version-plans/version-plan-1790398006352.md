Skeleton avatar, button and input placeholders now default to 32px high (sm 24px, lg 40px) to match the control sizes, and the circular skeleton is 32px. Skeleton button and input placeholders accept a `width` (numbers are pixels) to match the control they stand in for; `block` still stretches them to full width.

AndesSkeleton motion is calmer: the active shimmer sweeps over 1.8s with a softer band (a hard flash on the dark theme before), and the pulse dips only to 60% opacity.

AndesSkeleton title and paragraph bars now sit on the same lines as the text they stand in for - a 16px title bar in a 24px line, 14px body rows on a 22px pitch, a 4px step between them - so content no longer jumps when it loads.
