Add `AndesVideoPlayer`, an accessible media player for video and audio, on demand and live: native media element, signal store, auto-hiding control bar, captions, chapters, storyboard previews, speed, quality and audio-track menus, keyboard shortcuts, touch gestures, background and audio-only modes, and an adapter contract for adaptive streaming engines. Every control is also a standalone `andes-player-*` part for custom layouts.
