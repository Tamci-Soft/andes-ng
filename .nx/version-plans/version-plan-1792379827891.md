Add `AndesVideoPlayer`, an accessible media player for video and audio, on demand and live: native media element, signal store, auto-hiding control bar, captions, chapters, storyboard previews, speed, quality and audio-track menus, keyboard shortcuts, touch gestures, background and audio-only modes, and an adapter contract for adaptive streaming engines. Every control is also a standalone `andes-player-*` part for custom layouts.

Playlists with previous/next, repeat and shuffle, an up-next countdown and a playlist menu; a caption style menu (size, colours, background opacity, edges, font) remembered with `persistKey`; resume where the viewer left off; automatic reconnection after network failures; an opt-in floating mini player; keyboard help (`?`), frame stepping, chapter jumps and a right-click player menu; built-in labels in Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian.
