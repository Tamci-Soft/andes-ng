Add `AndesCascader`: a select-like field that opens a panel with one column of options per level, for picking a path through a tree. It supports multiple selection with tags and checkboxes, `changeOnSelect`, click or hover expansion, options loaded on demand with `loadData`, search across every path with highlighted matches, custom display and field names, and tree keyboard navigation (arrows to move, open and close columns, Enter to pick) with a combobox that keeps focus.
