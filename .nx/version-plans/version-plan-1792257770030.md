An open Tooltip now stays aligned with its trigger when the trigger or the tooltip changes size, and exposes `updatePosition()` to re-align it after other layout shifts.

Tooltip text moves from 13px to the 12px caption size, and the tooltip enters with a 150ms fade and a 4px drift, like the other overlays.

Tooltip gains a `variant="inverse"` dark surface with light text in both themes, the success, info and warning colour presets now meet 4.5:1 text contrast, and the tooltip fades and drifts back toward its trigger when it closes.
