Add a shared overlay behavior primitive (`AndesOverlayPrimitive` plus trigger/content/close directives) that Dialog, Drawer/Sheet, Popover, Tooltip and Dropdown Menu build on: portal rendering, anchored/centered/edge positioning with collision handling, optional focus trap and focus restoration, independently toggleable Escape and click-outside dismissal, scroll lock, and z-index/scrim wired to the `--andes-z-index-*` and `--andes-color-overlay` tokens. Closing supports exit animations: the pane, its direct element children and any backdrop get a `data-andes-closing` attribute (exported as `ANDES_OVERLAY_CLOSING_ATTRIBUTE`) and stop receiving pointer events, and the overlay detaches once the animations that attribute starts have finished, at most 300ms later (`ANDES_OVERLAY_EXIT_TIMEOUT_MS`); without an exit animation, under reduced motion or without animation support the close stays synchronous. `isOpen()` turns false immediately, focus restoration (skipped if the user moved focus elsewhere on the page during the exit) and `closed` follow the detach, and re-opening during the exit cancels it on the same pane.
