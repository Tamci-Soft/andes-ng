# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback and focus discussion on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation and publishing another person's private information.

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues or contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or severe violations. Reports should be sent privately to the security or governance contact that will be published with the upstream repository. Until that contact exists, do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.
